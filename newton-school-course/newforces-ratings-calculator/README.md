## Problem Statement

In the Newforces contest, participants ratings can increase or decrease based on the contest type they participate in and whether they hack any solutions. The new rating is calculated based on following rules:

 Div. 2 (Type 2): Rating increases by 80 points.

 Educational (Type 3): Rating increases by 50 points.

 
 

 If the participant&#39;s rating is between 1200 and 2000 (inclusive):
 
 Div. 1 (Type 1): Rating increases by 100 points.

 Div. 2 (Type 2): Rating remains the same.

 Educational (Type 3): Rating decreases by 30 points.

 
 

 If the participant&#39;s rating is above 2000:
 
 Div. 1 (Type 1): Rating increases by 50 points.

 Div. 2 (Type 2): Rating decreases by 30 points.

 Educational (Type 3): Rating decreases by 100 points.

 
 

Rules based on whether the user has hacked any solution or not:

 If the user has hacked any solution: Applies a further increase of 50 points to the rating.

 Did Not Hack: No additional change is applied.
