## Problem Statement

You are given two non-negative integers. Your task is to add the digits of the two numbers at corresponding positions (from right to left, i.e., least significant digit to most significant digit) and find the sum of two numbers.

Write a program to compute and print the resulting number formed by these digit-wise sums.

### Input
The first and only line of input contains two space-separated integers a and b.

### Output
Print an integer, the number formed by these digit-wise sums.

### Example
Input
543 4592
Output
5135
Explanation
We align the digits from right to left:
0543
4592
Now add digit-wise from right to left:
3 + 2 = 5
4 + 9 = 13 → take 3 and put 1 in carry.
5 + 5 + 1 (carry) = 11 → take 1 and put 1 in carry.
0 + 4 + 1 (carry) = 5
Now reverse the result to form the final number:
5 1 3 5 → 5135
