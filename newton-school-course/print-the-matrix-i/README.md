## Problem Statement

You are given a 2D matrix mat of dimensions n &times; m. Your task is to print the matrix in a way such that each of the n rows contains m space-separated integers.

### Input
The first line of input contains two space-separated integers representing the values n and m respectively.
Each of the next n line contains m space-separated integers, where the i-th line represents the i-th row.

### Output
Print n rows, each containing m space-separated integers, representing the input matrix mat.

### Example
Input
3 2
1 2
3 4
5 6
Output
1 2
3 4
5 6

Input
3 4
9 9 9 9
9 9 9 9
9 9 9 9
Output
9 9 9 9
9 9 9 9
9 9 9 9

## Solution

```js
// ─── 2 ───
n ,m = map(int,input().split())

matrix = []
for i in range(n):
    l1 = list(map(int,input().split()))
    matrix.append(l1)

for i in range(n):
    for j in range(m):
        print(matrix[i][j],end=" ")
    print()

// ─── 8 ───
1 2 
3 4 
5 6
```
