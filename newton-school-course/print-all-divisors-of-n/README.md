## Problem Statement

You are given a positive integer N. Your task is to find and print all the positive divisors of N in increasing order.

Note: A divisor of N is a number d such that d divides N completely.

 

### Input
The first and only line of input contains a single integer N.

### Output
Print space-separated integers, representing the positive divisors of N in increasing order.

### Example
Input
10
Output
1 2 5 10
Explanation
A divisor of 10 is any number that divides 10 exactly, i.e., with no remainder.
Let’s check which numbers divide 10:
1 → 10 ÷ 1 = 10 → ✅ (no remainder)
2 → 10 ÷ 2 = 5 → ✅
3 → 10 ÷ 3 = 3.33… → ❌
4 → 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5 → ❌
5 → 10 ÷ 5 = 2 → ✅
6 to 9 → None divide 10 evenly
10 → 10 ÷ 10 = 1 → ✅

## Solution

```js
N = int(input())
for i in range(1,N+1):
    if  N%i==0:
        print(i, end=" ")
```
