## Problem Statement

You are given two values:

 * age &mdash; the age of a person

 * income &mdash; the annual income of the person (in rupees)

Your task is to calculate the amount of tax that the person needs to pay.

The tax rate depends on both the person&#39;s age and annual income.

Print the tax amount that must be paid.

### Input
The first line contains two space-separated integers age and income.

### Output
Print a single integer representing the tax amount.

### Example

Input

Output

80000

age, income = map(int, input().split())

if age < 18:
    if income <= 1000000:
