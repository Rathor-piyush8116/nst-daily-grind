## Problem Statement

Ram is planning his holidays and visits to his hometown. He keeps a list of upcoming holidays and updates it regularly as he makes plans or learns about new holidays. Ram&#39;s initial list of holidays is: [&#39;New Year&#39;, &#39;Republic Day&#39;, &#39;Holi&#39;, &#39;God Froday&#39;, &#39;Independence Day&#39;, &#39;Diwali&#39;, &#39;Eid&#39;].

Write a program to help Ram perform the following tasks and display the list after each update:

1. Updating a Holiday Name:

 * Ram notices a mistake in the holiday list, where "God Froday" is incorrectly listed.

 * He corrects it to "Good Friday", updating the fourth holiday in the list.

2. Adding New Holidays:

 * Ram learns about "Eid" and decides to add it to his list at the end.

 * He also includes "Makar Sankranti" in the third position as it&#39;s an important festival for him.

3. Removing Holidays:

 * Ram discovers that "Eid" appears twice in the list, so he removes the last occurrence of "Eid".

 * He also decides to remove "Independence Day" because he will be attending the flag hoisting at his office instead of visiting his hometown.

Note:

After every operation, print the current state of the holiday list.
