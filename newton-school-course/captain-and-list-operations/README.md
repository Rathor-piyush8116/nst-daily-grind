## Problem Statement

Once upon a time in the Kingdom of Codehaven, there lived a legendary adventurer known as Captain Python.

Captain Python was renowned for his mastery of Pythonic arts&mdash;skills that helped him navigate through the most difficult programming challenges Captain Python had a lot of members in his army and he started his tour for winning the ocean.

Help the captain to solve the challenges which he will be facing in his journey 

Challenge 1: The Captain met with his lost brother and now they want to combine their army to form a stronger one, concatenate the two lists of armies to form a new army, and print the new army list. 

Challenge 2: During their journey towards the West Captain Python finds a mysterious potion that can help them multiply their team size by 3 . Print the new army formed after the consumption of the mysterious portion. Here we are considering the initial list as the one which is formed by concatenation from challenge 1.

Challenge 3: Captain Python wants to know whether they have an "Archer" in their team or not. Print True if he has an archer in their team or otherwise print False

Challenge 4: Captain got some news from his informers that there is some "Enemy" in their army. Print "Safe" if "Enemy" is not present in the army, otherwise print "Not Safe".

Note : Challenges are solved in sequential manner and are interrelated to each other.

### Input
The first line of input consists of multiple space-separated strings, representing the members of Captain python army.
The second line also contains multiple space-separated strings, representing of the member of Captain python's brother army.

### Output

* First line of output is the army formed after combining the armies of Captain and his brother.

* Second line of output is the army formed after consumption of Mysterious Potion.

* Third line of output is True or False depending upon whether the "Archer" is present in army or not. 

* Fourth line of output is "Safe" or "Not Safe" depending upon whether the "Enemy" is present in army or not.

### Example

Archer Captain

True
Safe

# Challenge 3: Check if "Archer" is present in the army
