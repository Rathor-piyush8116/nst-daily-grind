## Problem Statement

The bus driver at NST Campus is planning his daily pickup route. He needs to pick up students from two hostels: Hostel 1 and Hostel 2, and return to the NST campus afterward. To save fuel, he wants to choose the route that results in the minimum total distance traveled.

There are three roads available:

 
 A road of length d1 connects the NST campus and Hostel 1

 

 
 A road of length d2 connects the NST campus and Hostel 2

 

 
 A road of length d3 directly connects Hostel 1 and Hostel 2

 

The driver can travel on any road any number of times, and in any direction. He must start at NST campus, pick up students from both hostels, and return back to the campus, while minimizing the total distance.

Please refer to the following image for further clarity:

### Input
A single line contains three integers:

 * d1 — the distance between NST campus and Hostel 1
 * d2 — the distance between NST campus and Hostel 2
 * d3 — the distance between Hostel 1 and Hostel 2
 

### Output
Print a single integer — the minimum total distance the bus must travel.

### Example
Example 1:
Input
4 6 2

Output
12

Explanation:
Try all possible loops:
* Campus → Hostel 1 → Hostel 2 → Campus: 4 + 2 + 6 = 12

* Campus → Hostel 2 → Hostel 1 → Campus: 6 + 2 + 4 = 12

* Campus → Hostel 1 → Campus → Hostel 2 → Campus: 4 + 4 + 6 + 6 = 20, not minimal

So, minimum = 12

Example 2:
Input
2 2 8

Output
8

## Solution

# Your code here
d1, d2, d3 = map(int,input().split())
route1 = d1 + d2 + d3
route2 = 2 * d1 + 2 * d2
route3 = 2 * d1 + 2 * d3
route4 = 2 * d2 + 2 * d3
min_distance = min(route1, route2, route3, route4)
print(min_distance)
