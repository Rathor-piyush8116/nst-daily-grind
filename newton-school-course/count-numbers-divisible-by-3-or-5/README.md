## Problem Statement

You are given a positive integer N. Your task is to print all numbers in the range from 1 to N (inclusive) that are divisible by 3 or 5, but not both.

### Input
The first and only line of input contains a positive integer N.

### Output
Print all numbers in the range from 1 to N (inclusive) that are divisible by 3 or 5, but not both.

### Example
Input
15
Output
3 5 6 9 10 12
Explanation
From 1 to 15, numbers like 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, and 12 are divisible by 3 or 5, but not both.
15 is divisible by both, so it's excluded.
