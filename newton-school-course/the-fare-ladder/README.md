## Problem Statement

You are given the distance travelled by a passenger on a city bus and the passenger&#39;s age. The bus fare depends on the distance travelled, with different fare slabs applied for different ranges of distance.

The fare is calculated according to the following rules:

 * If the distance is 5 km or less, the fare is ₹10.

 * If the distance is more than 5 km but not more than 15 km, the fare is ₹20.

 * If the distance is more than 15 km but not more than 30 km, the fare is ₹35.

 * If the distance is more than 30 km, the fare is ₹50.

Passengers aged 60 years or above receive a 50% discount on the applicable fare. Given the distance travelled and the passenger&#39;s age, calculate and print the final fare after applying the discount, if applicable.

### Input
The first and only line of input contains two space-separated integers:

* distance — the distance travelled by the passenger in kilometres.

* age — the age of the passenger in years.

### Output
Print the final fare after applying the applicable age-based discount.

If the final fare is a decimal value, print the decimal value as it is.

### Example
Input:
20 65

Output:
17.5

Explanation:
The passenger travels 20 km, so the applicable fare is ₹35.

Since the passenger is 65 years old, they qualify for a 50% discount.

Final fare = 35 × 50 / 100 = 17.5

Therefore, the final fare is 17.5.

Input:
10 25

Output:
20

Explanation:
The passenger travels 10 km, so the applicable fare is ₹20.

Since the passenger is younger than 60 years, no discount is applied.

Therefore, the final fare is 20.

distance, age = map(int,input().split())
ans=10
if distance<=5 :
    ans=10
elif 5<distance<=15: 
    ans=20
elif 15<distance<=30:  
    ans=35
elif distance>30:
    ans=50

if age>=60:
    print(ans/2)
else:
    print(ans)
