## Problem Statement

A shopkeeper wants to generate a simple digital bill for a customer. You are given the customer&#39;s name and the prices of three products purchased by the customer.

Your task is to calculate the total price of the three products and print the bill in the exact format specified.

The customer&#39;s name should be displayed after Customer:. The three product prices should be displayed on the same line, separated by |. Finally, print the sum of all three prices after Total:Rs..

### Input

* The first line contains the customer's name.

* The second line contains three space-separated integers representing the prices of the three products.

### Output
Print the bill in the following format:

Customer:Name
Prices:Price1|Price2|Price3
Total:Rs.Total

Replace Name with the customer's name, Price1, Price2, and Price3 with the given prices, and Total with their sum.

### Example
Input:
Adarsh
120 250 80

Output:
Customer:Adarsh
Prices:120|250|80
Total:Rs.450

Input:
Rahul
500 150 350

Output:
Customer:Rahul
Prices:500|150|350
Total:Rs.1000

## Solution

```js
// ─── 2 ───
name = input()
p1, p2, p3= map(int,input().split())
total = p1+p2+p3
print("Customer:",name,sep="")
print("Prices:",p1,"|", p2,"|",p3,sep="")
print("Total:Rs.",total,sep="")

// ─── 31 ───

Prices:120|250|80
```
